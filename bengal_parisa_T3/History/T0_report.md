# Iran conflict and Strait of Hormuz
Checkpoint: T0
Evidence cutoff: 2026-03-05T23:59:59Z

## BLUF

The Iran conflict has disrupted the Strait of Hormuz, a critical global oil and gas chokepoint, prompting warnings, market disruption, and outside efforts to keep shipping moving.

## Key Developments

Iran has declared the Strait of Hormuz closed and threatened to attack any ship attempting to pass, but U.S. Central Command said the strait remains open.

Iranian forces have issued radio warnings and reported attacks on vessels, prompting some international shipping groups to suspend sailings in the waterway.

The United States said it will provide insurance guarantees and naval escorts for oil tankers and other vessels transiting the strait.

Operational disruption has left hundreds of tankers blocked in the Gulf, with Reuters reporting shipping costs rising and one report citing more than 540 tankers carrying over 300 million barrels of oil.

China has urged all parties to maintain security in the strait and said it will take measures to ensure its own energy security.
