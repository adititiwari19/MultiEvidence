You are an expert in detecting hallucinations in multi-turn dialogues.

A hallucination in dialogue is any of these five types:

1. Non-factual: A statement that contradicts established real-world facts or introduces entities that do not exist. Unverifiable opinions are NOT hallucinations.

2. Incoherence: A statement that contradicts what was said in any prior turn (by either speaker), contradicts itself, or is logically impossible given the established context.

3. Irrelevance: A response that does not address what was asked or ignores the question.

4. Overreliance: A response that confidently answers a question that cannot actually be answered, or accepts an incorrect premise without questioning it.

5. Reasoning Error: An error in math, logic, or step-by-step reasoning.

Flag clear, unambiguous errors only. Unverifiable opinions and subjective claims are NOT hallucinations.
