# T3 QA question ideas

## Some possible questions:

- What does the evidence say about whether the Strait of Hormuz is open or blocked as of March 19?
- Which sources support the report's claims about shipping disruption and oil prices?
- What do the reports say about Japan's response to the shipping disruption?
- Which statements in the current report are uncertain or require additional sourcing?

## Update-aware question (requires previous checkpoint context)

- What changed in the report between March 10 and March 19, and which new sources support those edits?

## Careful-handling question

- Can we confirm from these sources alone that the strait was formally closed to every vessel throughout the entire period?

Please check generated answers against the supplied articles before treating any audit flag or lack of flag as correct. The demo should not depend on known false-positive audit explanations.
